An extension of [Color Palooza](/work/color-palooza). After finishing that exploration, we had a bunch of small project ideas for visualizing what we'd found and sharing it with others, both creatively and literally. Paint Palooza is where those ideas are getting built out.

First up is a paint app modeled after Windows 95 Paint, but built to let you switch color spaces and watch the same color shift in real time across different historical perspectives.
