I'm always dreaming up trip itineraries, and when I travel I end up with a long list of places to eat and things to see. 

I used to save them all in Apple/Google Maps, but I wanted a cuter place to keep them and a better way to share them with friends, so I built _Wanderland_. 

It's a travel journal where you pin where you've been and where you want to go on a hand-drawn globe, and bring your friends into your world so you can see each other's spots.

It's invite only for now while I work out the kinks with my friends, but you can explore the home page and request access. If there's room, I just might let you in (:
