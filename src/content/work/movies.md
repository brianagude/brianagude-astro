I watch a lot of movies and I got tired of flipping through streaming platforms one by one trying to find something new worth watching.

During Covid I built a site where my friends could submit movies for virtual watch parties, so this is the evolved version of that idea, but built for me.

You click a button and get a movie. A lot of the time it's something you wouldn't have found on your own. It pulls from The Movie Database and gives you enough context to actually decide if it's worth your time.
