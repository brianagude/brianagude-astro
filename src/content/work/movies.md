I watch a lot of movies and got tired of flipping through streaming platforms trying to find something new worth watching.

During Covid I built a site where friends could submit movies for virtual watch parties. This is the evolved version of that idea, built just for me.

Click a button, get a movie. Usually something you wouldn't have found on your own. It pulls from The Movie Database and gives you enough context to decide if it's actually worth your time.
