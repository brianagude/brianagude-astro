As I was requesting my absentee ballot, the idea for this site came back to me. 

I’m not super into politics unless it’s a name I already know, like Keisha Lance Bottoms, Mayor Andre Dickens, Obama, Zohran Mamdani etc. I usually have no idea who’s running, what they’re running for, or what they’ve actually been doing. I couldn’t tell you who’s in Congress or what they’re working on, and neither could most of my friends. And I’ve always wanted to build something that fixes that. 

So on this day I finally did, and it turned out _soo_ cute!

I also learned that most of the Democrats on my ballot this year are Black women which also put the biggest smile on my face. ((((:

So, _Please Vote_!
