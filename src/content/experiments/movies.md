I watch a lot of movies and I got tired of flipping through streaming platforms one by one trying to find something worth watching. During Covid I built a site where friends could submit movies for our virtual watch parties.

This is the evolved version of that idea, but built for one person: _me_.

You click a button and get a movie. Something new, something old, something you probably wouldn't have found on your own. It pulls from The Movie Database and gives you enough context to actually decide if it's worth your time. The part I loved building most was figuring out the different algorithms for how to surface results that felt relevant to my taste in movies.
