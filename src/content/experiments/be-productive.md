I've tried everything. Notion, Linear, every productivity app with a waitlist and a manifesto about focus. They all had too much upkeep, or were missing the one thing I actually needed, and I kept finding myself stitching together three subscriptions just to feel okay about my day.

So I started writing notes instead. Every morning or night, just what I got done, what I still needed to do, what was coming next. It was ugly but it worked. And that became the blueprint for Be Productive.

It's a web app built around the way I actually think — quick capture, time tracking, recurring tasks, a Pomodoro timer, a GitHub-style activity heatmap so I can see that yes, I did do things this week even when it doesn't feel like it. I've been using it consistently since I built it, updating it in the shape of what I actually need, and somewhere along the way other people started using it too.
