I've tried so many different productivity apps.

Notion, Linear, everything with a manifesto about focus. For me, they all had too much upkeep, or were missing something I actually needed, and I kept finding myself having to restart with a new app that promised a better process.

So I started writing notes instead.

Every morning or night, I'd write I got done, what I still needed to do, and what was coming next. It was messy, but it worked. And that became the blueprint for Be Productive.

It's a web app built around the way I actually think ~ quick notes, time tracking, recurring tasks, and a stats page so I can see that yes, I _did_ do things this week, even when it doesn't feel like it. I've been using it consistently since I built it, updating it in the shape of what I actually need, and now other people have started to adopt it too!
